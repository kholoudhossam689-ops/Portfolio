Mithaq

Making freelance pricing and client–freelancer decisions more transparent.

UI/UX Design Web Development ASP.NET MVC
The Problem

A Lack of Transparency

The broader problem is a lack of transparency between the two sides: What is a fair price? Who is the right freelancer? How can both sides make a confident decision?

For Freelancers

Freelancers often struggle to determine a fair price for their work. Pricing too low can undervalue their skills, while pricing too high can make their service less competitive.

For Clients

Clients often struggle to understand what a realistic budget should be when looking for freelance services. They also need a way to compare freelancers beyond price alone.

"What if a freelance platform could make pricing and decision-making more transparent for both sides?"

Mithaq was designed around this idea. Instead of leaving freelancers to guess their prices and clients to guess whether a quote is reasonable, the platform introduces structured pricing, comparison, AI-assisted evaluation, and trust signals into the workflow.

Helping Freelancers Price Their Work

AI Pricing Engine

Problem: Freelancers need guidance when deciding what to charge for a project.

Solution: Mithaq calculates a recommended project price based on relevant factors such as freelancer experience, certifications, project complexity, estimated effort, urgency, demand, and market benchmarks. The goal is not to replace judgment, but to provide a more informed starting point.

Giving Clients a Clearer Budget

Service Pricing Distribution

Problem: Clients may not know what a realistic budget is for a particular service.

Solution: Mithaq presents a market-based price range using a structured Budget → Standard → Premium tiering. This gives clients a clearer understanding of the pricing landscape before choosing a service or freelancer.

Comparing Proposals Beyond Price

Quote Comparison

Problem: Clients may receive multiple proposals, but price alone is not enough to make a confident decision.

Solution: Mithaq allows clients to compare proposals using holistic factors such as price, delivery time, freelancer experience, and AI evaluation, streamlining the decision-making pipeline.

Understanding the Market

AI Market Analytics

Problem: Freelancers and clients can make pricing decisions without a clear view of current market conditions.

Solution: Mithaq provides category-specific insights (demand levels, average hourly rates, pricing benchmarks, market trends) to support decision-making processes.

Helping Clients Discover Talent

Talent Network

Problem: Clients need ways to discover suitable freelancers beyond simply receiving proposal lists.

Solution: Clients can explore freelancer skills, experience, and external portfolios via a dedicated talent network to make more informed hiring decisions.

The Core Workflow

Project Workspace

Mithaq structures the active project phase to reduce risk and maintain context.

Escrow Payment Flow

Mithaq structures project payments around approved milestones, helping reduce uncertainty between both sides before funds are released.

Built-in Chat

Keeps project-related communication connected to the workflow so important discussions remain associated with the project.

Designing a Complex Two-Sided Platform

UI/UX Design Decisions

One of the main challenges was presenting a large number of features without overwhelming the user.

Information Hierarchy

Organizing pricing, freelancer information, proposals, and project details so users can scan and compare information quickly.

Trust & Transparency

Making pricing, experience, reviews, and other decision-making information visible at the right moments in the user journey.

Role-Based Experiences

Designing different experiences for freelancers and clients while keeping the overall product visually consistent.

Reusable UI Patterns

Using consistent cards, buttons, navigation, forms, tables, badges, and information blocks throughout the platform for scalability.

Reflection

What I Learned

Mithaq challenged me to think beyond individual screens and design a complete product experience for two different user roles.

I learned how to organize complex information, design decision-making flows, and balance functionality with clarity across a web platform.

The project also helped me understand how AI-assisted features can be incorporated into a product experience without making the interface feel unnecessarily complicated.
